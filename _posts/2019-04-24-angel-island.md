For my wife's birthday, we decided to take the ferry to Angel Island, a place we'd never been; just
another of those places we'd heard of but never gone to because... well, it's always there and
there's just always something else to do. You know, like San Franciscans and Alcatraz.

![An interesting structure](angel-island.jpg){: lqip=angel-island.lqip.jpg }
_Not sure what this structure is, probably some form of watchtower thing, but we liked the looks of it_

Any rate, the island ended up being pretty interesting and having some historical sites, as well as
being a decent hiking area. To highlight the history found on the [Angel Island Conservancy website][1],
the island was originally a fishing and hunting site for Miwok Indians, because a haven for the
Spanish, a US Army post starting during the Civil War, an immigrant processing site from 1910-1940,
a Nike Missile site in the 1950's and 1960's, and then eventually turned into a State Park.

![Camp Reynolds Hospital](angel-island-2.jpg){: lqip=angel-island-2.lqip.jpg }
_Camp Reynolds Hospital, a hospital in the Civil War era West Garrison_

Nowadays, the island is mostly open to the public as well as for groups to do various types of
learning. For instance, while we were there, there was a group of Boy Scouts who were being taught
how to work in one of the Civil War-era kitchens, using techniques that would have been used during
that time. Gotta say, it was pretty amusing hearing the tweens and teens complaining about the work!

![Camp Reynolds](angel-island-3.jpg){: lqip=angel-island-3.lqip.jpg }
_The West Garrison with which the Camp Reynolds Hospital was associated_

We weren't really prepared for the size of the island; we didn't have the time to go all the way
around, but it worked out because it was such a nice day that it was getting a bit too warm to do
that much walking. Quite a lot of the walking was done on paved roads, which made walking a bit
easier, but definitely didn't help with the temperature

![Throwing some rocks](angel-island-4.jpg){: lqip=angel-island-4.lqip.jpg }
_Look... it's water_

After a nice picnic snack and a couple hours of walking, we took the ferry back to San Francisco
then headed back home. Altogether, we enjoyed the day, though getting an overcast day might be
nicer next time, at least for temperature purposes! Also, next time, we'll be sure to go there
during prime wildflower season and perhaps take our bikes!

Thanks for reading!
