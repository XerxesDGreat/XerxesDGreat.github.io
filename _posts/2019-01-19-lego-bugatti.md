I got it; the next large Lego car, the [Bugatti Chiron][1]!

Just like the [Porsche 911 GT3 RS]({% post_url 2017-11-20-porsche-timelapse-assembly %}), this thing
is huge and took quite some time to assemble. The thing that amazes me about all these builds is 
how mechanically intricate they are; full-on transmissions with reverse and several sequential gears,
full on steering and suspension, etc.

![Mechanisms](lego-bugatti-2.jpg){: lqip=lego-bugatti-2.lqip.jpg }

![Moar Mechanisms](lego-bugatti-4.jpg){: lqip=lego-bugatti-4.lqip.jpg }

It's also impressive how nice the boxes are; very well put together. Almost makes me feel bad for
tossing them as I always do. _Almost_.

![Box](lego-bugatti-1.jpg){: lqip=lego-bugatti-1.lqip.jpg }

Any rate, after the whole thing is put together, it's an incredible model to display and to play
around with a little bit. I got a video showing off the pertinent parts, with a 7-year-old for scale
